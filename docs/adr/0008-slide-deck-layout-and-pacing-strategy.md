# 0008: Slide Deck Layout and Pacing Strategy

## Context
When translating proposals into PowerPoint, jamming multiple dense topics onto a single slide results in unreadable text and poor pitch effectiveness.

## Decision
We decided to design the PPTX compiler to emit a 15-20 slide presentation following executive pitch pacing: 1 concept or data visualization per slide, utilizing 16:9 widescreen layout with distinct visual hierarchy, vector card grids, and high-contrast styling.

## Why
Aligns with real-world executive presentation dynamics where clarity and visual pacing drive stakeholder conviction and ease live pitching.
